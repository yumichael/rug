How the pattern works

Diagonal integer coordinates are combined with XOR. OR combines straight or diagonal coordinates after squaring or taking fourth powers. A modular post-power creates the pattern. Odd multipliers and offsets give its colors. The field is a centered crop of a square; each border is one continuous periodic pattern with square corners.

Pattern rotations turn a region independently; Rotate rug turns the whole preview and PNG export. A border motif repeats at its width, though some powers create smaller repeats. Images up to 16,777,216 pixels render at full resolution; larger images show sampled cells until you export the full rug.
